Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 10, 2026

1. Introduction

REGGY ("we", "our", "us") is a platform for creating short-form video content using AI. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2. Information We Collect

Account information: email address and password when you register.

Connected accounts: when you connect YouTube, Instagram, or TikTok, we store OAuth tokens to publish videos on your behalf. We do not store your social media passwords.

Generated content: images, videos, and audio created through the platform.

Usage data: basic interaction logs to improve the service.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the service
  • To publish content to your connected social media accounts
  • To generate AI-powered video content based on your inputs
  • To improve the platform

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google/YouTube API: for publishing videos and managing channels. Subject to Google Privacy Policy.
  • OpenAI: for text generation (prompts, metadata).
  • fal.ai: for image, video, and music generation.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers. OAuth tokens are stored encrypted. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Your Rights

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us. You can disconnect social media accounts at any time through the platform.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy

REGGY's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
